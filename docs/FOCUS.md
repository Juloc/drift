# Drift: focus (interview with the owner, 2026-11-25 evening)

No more feature sprawl. First ONE map and ONE car, really good; then perfect them.

## What the first solid version is
- **Core:** drift with friends. A public lobby AND private sessions by link. 16+ players per world.
- **Map:** exactly one, the **touge at dusk** (model: the mountain pass roads of Gunma; no names or references from anime or games): an open plaza by a crater lake on top as the meeting place (free space), from there the **narrow** pass road down with hairpins, torii, stone lanterns, cedars, guardrails, chevrons; one run takes **about 90 s**. At the bottom a turn / way back (or a teleport back to the top).
- **Car:** exactly one, perfect (model, paint, lights, rims, sound, handling numbers). Proposal: AE86 Trueno.
- **Feel of driving:** switchable Arcade/Sim, both good. Everything matters: turn-in and breaking traction, holding with throttle, left-right transitions, sense of speed, sound, smoke and marks.
- **Controls:** a PC keyboard is the yardstick.
- **Camera:** swings with the drift (shows the angle).
- **Walls:** hard, cost a lot of speed.
- **Scoring:** angle and speed plus zones and line as in a competition (clipping zones/points along the track).
- **Tandem:** only a bonus (a multiplier when drifting close together), no mode of its own.
- **Look and gameplay equally important.**

## Paused (stays in the code, not visible or not developed further)
More maps, more cars, challenges, races. Also: tuning extensions, creative mode extensions, new menus.

## Way of working
- Small playable previews every week rather than big leaps; the owner checks on a PC with a keyboard.
- Look session: only the touge look (after the touge mockup) and the shared look modules.
- Drift session: touge map (terrain, road, plaza), zone/line scoring, walls, camera, feel, lobby 16+ with link.
- Car session: one car, perfect; the other models wait on their branch and do not enter the game.
