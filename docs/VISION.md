# Vision: Drift

Status: **confirmed by the owner in an interview, 2026-11-26.** This page says what the game is meant to become; [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) says in which order, [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) what is being built now.

## The game in one paragraph

You open the game and arrive in a **public open world** (the meeting point): a small, beautiful place with mountain roads where everybody who is online drives around together, shows off, drifts, starts races and drives in tandem. Next to it there are individual **tracks**. Meeting, racing and drift points are **equally important**. The cars are **real, recognisable cars** you can shape like in Forza or Need for Speed. Everything runs on **our own server**.

## Look

**Low-poly sunset:** a flat-shaded low-poly world with soft colour gradients, a low sun, haze. The interface is calm like JulOS: glass cards, rounded corners, accent raspberry. Cars are realistic and recognisable (3D models from the pipeline) inside this stylised, clear world with good light. **At every step, pictures first for the owner, then build it out.**

## Ways to play

- **Open world:** drive freely, meet, emotes, start a race on the spot, drift for points.
- **Races and tracks:** lap races and sprints, time trial, boards per track.
- **Tandem (drifting side by side):** a **bonus while free driving** next to another player, **lead and chase** roles, a **tandem competition** (judged on both cars), and a **bot to practise** against.
- **Modes:** **Creative** (everything free) and **Story** (first *challenges with stars*; a career later).
- **Feel of driving:** adjustable. **Arcade is the default**; a switch gives fewer assists; boards show which feel a time was set with.
- **Controls and cameras:** keyboard and controller; several cameras (chase, bonnet, cockpit); steering wheel later.

## Cars and shaping

Forza/NFS level of shaping: paint, wheels, parts, body kits one by one, ride height and suspension, detailed values like Forza including a **performance class**, tuning; stickers later. Real cars come through the 3D pipeline on the owner's GPU PC (the S15 first) with Forza-like values per car (real-car numbers translated into our physics fields, never new physics rules).

## World, sound, people

Time of day and weather selectable, later dynamic. Sound: engine and tyres (first generated, later real recordings), music. Friends, private lobbies, crews, spectating. **32 and more players per world.**

## How we work

Small and often: every step starts with pictures for the owner, then it grows. Public from the first release; the account is the "Juloc Games" account for all games ([ACCOUNTS-GAMES.md](ACCOUNTS-GAMES.md)). The code stays small and readable, no build step in the browser, own models and own code first.

## Not now

Stickers, career, steering wheel, crews, spectating, dynamic weather: after the public start, in the order of the roadmap.
