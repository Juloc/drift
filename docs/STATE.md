## More steering angle (2026-11-26, the owner: "the drift game needs more steering angle")

`game/sim/physics.js`: `lockHard` 0.75 to **1.05 rad** (about 60 degrees, the wheels' limit; the counter-steer assist may use all of it), `lockMax` 0.55 to **0.80** (the player's lock at standstill, times the car's agility up to 1.25), `lockSpeed` 40 to **70** (the lock shrinks much less with speed). The wheel speed scales with the limit, so full lock still takes about the same 0.3 s; touch still reaches full lock (the ramp is untouched; the earlier headless two-finger run reads full steering after about a second of holding). The relay's `steer` byte now means +-1.05 rad (it was 0.75; `game/net/world.js` encodes and decodes it, `ARCHITECTURE.md` says so; the relay itself never reads it, so the website's copy is unaffected, only an old and a new page in one world would show each other's wheels at the wrong angle for a moment).

Before and after (`OLD_LOCK=1 node tools/measure.mjs` against `node tools/measure.mjs`):

| car | circle 20 km/h before → after (m) | wheel angle at 80 km/h, full steer (deg) | circle 50 km/h (m) | handbrake turn at 80, max slip (deg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dart | 15 → 14 | 25 → 44 | 32 → 32 | 70 → 71 |
| Pip | 14 → 14 | 31 → 52 | 33 → 33 | 88 → 88 |
| Bruno | 15 → 16 | 18 → 31 | 39 → 40 | 43 → 40 |
| Marlin | 18 → 17 | 24 → 42 | 39 → 39 | 66 → 56 |
| Ox | 11 → 12 | 16 → 29 | 27 → 27 | 49 → 48 |

What this means, honestly: the wheels now turn about **1.75 times as far at speed** (Dart at 80 km/h: 25 to 44 degrees) and the car can be caught in much deeper slides, but **the turning circles hardly change**: a tyre gives its most sideways force at about 11 degrees of slip (`alphaPeak` 0.2 rad) and less beyond it, so more wheel angle does not turn the car tighter by itself. Only at crawling speed does the angle itself limit the circle (15 to 14 m at 20 km/h). The five-cars test (`the five cars differ in the way their names promise`) passes unchanged and its reasons still hold: they compare cars with each other, and the order of power, top speed, turn-in and cornering grip did not move. If the owner wants the car to *turn tighter* (not just show more wheel angle), the lever is the tyre: a later peak (`alphaPeak` 0.2 to about 0.3) and a gentler fall after it; that changes the whole feel and needs a driver, so it was not done unasked. Picture with the bot drifting: `docs/screenshots/steer-orbit-wide.png` (from behind the wheel angle is hard to see; the bot steers into and against the slide). **Not checked:** a person driving it, a real phone.

## Milestone 3: races and best times (built 2026-11-26 on the orchestrator's go)

Only in the signed-in app (the public demo has no races). One race at a time, on the car park (the only map so far), up to 8 racers, 1 to 5 laps (the lobby button starts 3).

- **Flow** (as in ARCHITECTURE section 4, now built): anyone in the lobby presses "Start a race"; the others get an invitation (Join or No) for **10 s**, shorter once all have answered (at least 1 s); the starter alone is a time trial. The joined open a **race socket** (`/api/play/race/ws`, only for them, only during the grid phase), sit on their grid slot (the grid stands after the start line, so there is no run-up: a standing start), wait up to 10 s for each other, then **3, 2, 1, GO** (cars are locked until GO), the race, **20 s** for the others after the first finish, a **result table** (position, name, time, best lap, drift points, points; DNF for those who did not finish or left) for 15 s, back to the lobby (the lobby socket is reopened by the page), a pause of **20 s** before the next invitation.
- **The server decides** the start, the grid, gates and laps, positions and the result. Gates: 8 gates across the track (in `game/data/maps/01-car-park.json`, written by `tools/make-map.mjs`), crossed in order and in the direction of travel, crossing time interpolated between two poses; skipping gates or driving the wrong way counts for nothing; a lap faster than `loop length / (1.15 x top speed)` is not a time (the runner's results are marked unclean, no best time). **Drift points** are recomputed on the server from the poses by a C# port of the score (`DriftScore`), checked against the browser's by shared test vectors (`tests/vectors/drift-score.json`, made by `tools/make-score-vectors.mjs`). Points per race: positions 10-7-5-4-3-2-1-1 plus drift points / 100 (only finishers get position points).
- **Best times:** table `drift_best_time` (migration `002_best_times.sql`): best lap and best race per person and map; a time is written only if it beats the old one, only for finished, clean races, and the race time only for the map's standard 3 laps. `GET /api/times` and a card in the garage show the best 5 of each (nickname or neutral name, car).
- **How it is built:** `server/Relay/WorldRelay.cs` got a `PoseAccepted` hook (queued, never called back into), `Session.Peer` public and `ITextPeer`; `RelayEndpoint.Serve` takes text frames (small JSON, 512 bytes) for the control messages. The race has its own relay world (`WorldRelay`, at most 8, the same checks as everywhere (since the review of 2026-11-26 including walls, obstacles and frame numbers: see ARCHITECTURE section 4)) and `RaceHub` (`src/Drift/Race/`) is the state machine, driven 10 times a second; `RaceRun`/`RaceTrack` are pure logic; `RaceStore` the SQL. In the browser (`game/main.js`, only when the page says `data-race="1"`): the lobby socket is closed for the race socket and reopened afterwards; HUD with position, lap, time and drift; invitation banner, countdown, result table, leave button. The public demo page and the relay frames are unchanged.
- **Tests:** `scripts/test.sh`: **68 green** (new: gates and crossing geometry, three laps with sensible times, wrong way, shortcut, too-fast lap, order, the shared drift vectors, the hub end to end with fake clock and the relay's own checks: invitation and answers, one race at a time and the pause, grid and countdown, nobody joins outside the grid phase, a racer who never connects, a whole race with positions and drift and best times written, leaving = DNF, a race nobody finishes, poses before the start). `node --test game/`: 28 green.
- **Played** (headless, software graphics, published app, throwaway PostgreSQL, three throwaway accounts, an autopilot in each page): invitation seen by both others, grid, countdown, a one-lap race, result with a new best lap, the board API. Two of the three did not finish in time: the headless pages run at a few frames a second (slow motion), so they were still on the track when the 20 s after the first finish ran out; that is what DNF is for, but it means **a race of three finishing cars was not seen in a browser** (only in the hub tests). Pictures `docs/screenshots/m3-invite.png`, `m3-count.png`, `m3-race.png`, `m3-result.png`.

## How to run and check

- Serve the **repository root** (the page imports `../vendor/`): `python3 -m http.server 8150`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:8150/game/index.html`. Options in the address: `?debug=1` (frame time, triangles, draw calls), `?tier=low|medium|high`, `?touch=1` (show touch buttons on a desktop), `?demo=orbit&t=6` (a bot drives a drift circle, `t` seconds are simulated before the first frame), `?car=0..4` (the five cars' numbers are in `game/data/cars.json`; only Dart has a model so far, the others drive with the same body), `?lang=de`.
- Tests: `node --test game/` (20 tests, no packages): physics, collisions, score, loop, map file, car numbers. The numbers of how the cars drive: `node tools/measure.mjs`.
- The look probe: `http://127.0.0.1:8150/game/lookprobe.html` (`?tier=high|low`, `?touch=1`, `?view=overview`, `?view=cam&cam=x,y,z,lookX,lookY,lookZ`, `?t=seconds`, `?run=1` keeps driving, `?hud=plain|driver|off`, `?lang=de|en`, `?debug=1`); pictures and numbers: `node tools/shoot-probe.mjs <w> <h> <name> [--tier=..] [--touch] [--dpr=..] [--query=..] [--port=..]` (also measures the game page the same way with `--page=game/index.html`).
- Play in headless Chromium and write numbers and a picture: `node tools/play.mjs <w> <h> <name> [--touch] [--tier=..] [--secs=8] [--dpr=2]` (software graphics; it picks its own free DevTools port and kills its browser). ## Collision and damage (step 8, built 2026-11-26)

- **Cars are rectangles** (4.4 x 1.9 m, `game/sim/collision.js`, pure): rigid-body contact in 2D with the impulse at the contact point, so a hit **turns the car** (angular impulse; the inertia of a plate), the wall **brakes the sliding along it** (friction 0.35, so a glancing hit keeps most of the speed) and throws back the part into it at a restitution of 0.25; poles and planters are round obstacles. `collideBody` replaces the old circle test for your car in the game (the circle test of `sim/map.js` stays for the relay's checks and the bots). A straight hit on a flat face does not spin the car (the contact point is the middle of the face); every hit ends outside the wall (10 000 random hits tested).
- **Car against car:** `collideCars` with the cars' **weights** (`mass` in kg in `game/data/cars.json`: Dart 1200, Pip 900, Bruno 1600, Marlin 1350, Ox 1900; relative to 1200 kg; a heavy car is thrown about less and throws a light one more; weighted momentum is kept, tested) and friction 0.2. **Both games agree:** each game moves **only its own car** from the other's state (the latest poses, carried forward by the 140 ms interpolation delay), and the impulse is the same number on both sides (tested: it equals the full two-body result to 1e-9). Momentum is kept and energy never grows (tested). A far car (drawn simplified, interest areas) does not collide.
- **Damage:** the approach speed of a hit adds damage above 3 m/s (about 0.23 for a 10 m/s hit, at most 0.6 per hit, capped at 1); it heals slowly after 6 quiet seconds (a full repair in about four minutes) and **R repairs at once**. Effects: the body darkens, smoke comes from the nose over half, a small readout "Damage 35 %", and **a little less power (up to 25 %) and top speed (up to 10 %)**; there is a hit sound (`SoundSystem.bump`, a burst of low noise, louder with the hit). **Switch off:** key **V** or `?damage=0`, kept on the device; the creative mode will switch it off by itself. The damage of other cars is not sent (their look is the same for everybody), only yours is shown.
- **The server's checks hold:** `tools/make-collision-poses.mjs` crashes cars with the game's own physics and contact code into the planter, the outer wall, a pole, head on and side on, at 15 poses a second; `CollisionRelayTests` replays all of them through the relay with the car park's walls and obstacles: **every pose is accepted, nobody is kicked** (largest turn between two poses 0.43 rad against the relay's 1.29, largest move 1.5 m). No contact gives a car a jump of more than 40 m/s (a safety stop); the fastest car hitting a car at rest gives it about 24 m/s, which the relay believes for the slowest car (tested). The hit flag in the pose still ends a drift chain and a clean sheet.
- **Not done / not checked:** nobody drove it with a real second player (contact and rubber-banding under delay are only reasoned and tested in pure functions); no contact between a car and the practice bot; damage is not part of the drift score; the numbers (restitution, friction, damage per m/s) are first guesses that need driving; the relay does not yet know about contact at all (it only checks that poses stay plausible), so someone can still drive through another car with a modified page, as before.

## Sound (step 7, built 2026-11-26)

- **Own engine from loops (2026-11-26, the car session):** your own car plays loops at five fixed revs in two layers (on load, off load), as racing
  games play recordings: crossfaded by the revs (equal power), the pitch following them (playback rate), the layers mixed by the throttle, a
  70 ms cut at each upshift, a 13 Hz flutter at the limiter. Until recordings are allowed the loops are made by `game/sim/enginesynth.js`:
  one pressure pulse per firing (each cylinder a little different), the exhaust valve's ring, three pipe resonances, a silencer, a soft overdrive
  on load, intake noise in time with the firings; off load weak, late firings and crackle. The other cars keep the cheaper two oscillators.
  Car 1 (AE86) is now a high-revving four (900 to 7800 rpm). Proof without a loudspeaker: `node tools/sound/ramp.mjs 1 ae86` renders the
  game's own sound code in an OfflineAudioContext through idle, full throttle through the gears, the limiter and a lift, and writes
  `docs/sound/ae86-ramp.png` (spectrogram) and `docs/sound/ae86-ramp.wav` (13 s, listen to it). **Never listened to here.**

- **No files:** everything is made in the browser with WebAudio (`game/view/sound.js`), the numbers come from `game/sim/engine.js` (pure, tested). **Engine:** two oscillators (a sawtooth and a square one octave lower) through a low-pass filter; the revs come from a **virtual gearbox** (gears per car, the revs rise in a gear and fall at the shift, a little hysteresis), the pitch is the firing frequency (cylinders / 2 per revolution); the volume follows throttle and revs. **Each car sounds different** (`ENGINES`: Dart a lively four, Pip a small buzzy three, Bruno a low V8, Marlin a smooth six, Ox a heavy turbo diesel with a whistle when the throttle is lifted at high revs). **Tyres:** looped noise through a band-pass, louder and higher the further the rear slides (silent under a slide of 0.25 or at a crawl).

## Tandem (step 4, built 2026-11-26)

- **The judgement** (`game/sim/tandem.js`, C# port `src/Drift/Race/Tandem.cs`, shared vectors `tests/vectors/tandem.json` written by `tools/make-tandem-vectors.mjs`): two cars are in tandem while both drift the same way (slip over 15 degrees at over 8 m/s), at most 10 m apart, travelling within 0.45 rad of each other with slip angles within 0.4 rad; the quality q (0..1) is higher the closer (full at 4 m) and the more alike; the one ahead along the travel direction **leads**, the other **chases**, under 1 m along it is **side by side**. A chain of tandem seconds gives the **bonus multiplier** `1 + 0.5 q + 0.05 per second (to +0.5)`, at most x2, and bonus points `0.35 * speed * degrees^0.8 * (multiplier - 1)` per second (at most 1500), a break under 1 s is bridged, a chain ends banked.
- **On the server** (`TandemTracker`, hooked on the lobby relay's accepted poses): each car is judged against the nearest partner on the latest poses (nothing older than half a second); the car is told on a change, at most four times a second: `{t:"tandem", on, mult, role, q, with}` and `{t:"tandem.bank", points}`; the best banked chain of a signed-in person goes to the boards, **kind 3** (migration 012, `tandem` in `/api/times`); guests are judged and told but write nothing.
- **Tandem competition** (`TandemRun`, logic and board only, no page and no lobby messages yet): two rounds, A leads then B (3 s countdown, 4 s swap), the pair's score is the sum of what both earned: the lead its own slide rate, the chase the same rate times the quality while it is behind (a "chase" that is in front earns nothing); the pair's score goes to both signed-in drivers' boards, **kind 4** (migration 013).
- **Practice bot** (`game/sim/practice.js`): a bot that drives the orbit drift circle (found by trying: a steady circle at about 10 m/s on the car park without touching a wall) and the local judgement with the same functions; `?bot=1` or the key **B**; `?bot=near` starts it beside you; nothing is sent or kept. It knows only a circle: **an ideal line with angles per map comes with the maps of the open world.**
- **HUD:** a small chip "Tandem x1.4 - Lead" (`game/view/tandemhud.js`, texts German/English) and "Tandem bonus +N" when a chain is banked; the new HUD of the look session replaces the look, the messages stay.
- Tests: `TandemTests` (vectors, either side), `TandemTrackerTests` (lobby, told, banked, guest writes nothing, far and different ways earn nothing), `TandemRunTests`, in `game/sim/sim.test.mjs` the vectors, properties and the practice bot. The 64-driver timing test is loose now (p95 under 25 ms; it failed once at 10 ms while other test classes ran).

## Touge, slice 4 (zone scoring)
- `game/sim/zones.js` (pure, tests): clipping points (`clips` in the map file, here one at the inner edge of each of the eight hairpins, drawn as a white post with a pink glowing head) with a 9 m zone each. A drift through a zone pays when the car leaves it: value = (km/h / 10) x (degrees / 10) x 50 x closeness to the point; a clean clip (within 1.5 m) x1.5; each point pays once per run (R re-arms them). The bonus is added to the *pending* points of the drift chain (so the chain multiplies it and a hit or spin loses it); with no chain running it is lost. HUD: a "CLIP +N" or "Zone +N" toast.
- Client only: the server does not know the bonus (boards are paused), so a score with zone points is not comparable with the server's drift points. The line bot (a crude driver, 30 to 40 degrees, not on the line) earns about 170 to 300 per zone, a clean clip at 70 km/h and 35 degrees about 1800.
