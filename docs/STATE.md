## More steering angle (2026-11-26, the owner: "the drift game needs more steering angle")

`game/sim/physics.js`: `lockHard` 0.75 to **1.05 rad** (about 60 degrees, the wheels' limit; the counter-steer assist may use all of it), `lockMax` 0.55 to **0.80** (the player's lock at standstill, times the car's agility up to 1.25), `lockSpeed` 40 to **70** (the lock shrinks much less with speed). The wheel speed scales with the limit, so full lock still takes about the same 0.3 s; touch still reaches full lock (the ramp is untouched; the earlier headless two-finger run reads full steering after about a second of holding). The relay's `steer` byte now means +-1.05 rad (it was 0.75; `game/net/world.js` encodes and decodes it, `ARCHITECTURE.md` says so; the relay itself never reads it, so the website's copy is unaffected, only an old and a new page in one world would show each other's wheels at the wrong angle for a moment).

## Collision and damage (step 8, built 2026-11-26)

- **Cars are rectangles** (4.4 x 1.9 m, `game/sim/collision.js`, pure): rigid-body contact in 2D with the impulse at the contact point, so a hit **turns the car** (angular impulse; the inertia of a plate), the wall **brakes the sliding along it** (friction 0.35, so a glancing hit keeps most of the speed) and throws back the part into it at a restitution of 0.25; poles and planters are round obstacles. `collideBody` replaces the old circle test for your car in the game (the circle test of `sim/map.js` stays for the relay's checks and the bots). A straight hit on a flat face does not spin the car (the contact point is the middle of the face); every hit ends outside the wall (10 000 random hits tested).
- **Car against car:** `collideCars` with the cars' **weights** (`mass` in kg in `game/data/cars.json`: Dart 1200, Pip 900, Bruno 1600, Marlin 1350, Ox 1900; relative to 1200 kg; a heavy car is thrown about less and throws a light one more; weighted momentum is kept, tested) and friction 0.2. **Both games agree:** each game moves **only its own car** from the other's state (the latest poses, carried forward by the 140 ms interpolation delay), and the impulse is the same number on both sides (tested: it equals the full two-body result to 1e-9). Momentum is kept and energy never grows (tested). A far car (drawn simplified, interest areas) does not collide.
- **Damage:** the approach speed of a hit adds damage above 3 m/s (about 0.23 for a 10 m/s hit, at most 0.6 per hit, capped at 1); it heals slowly after 6 quiet seconds (a full repair in about four minutes) and **R repairs at once**. Effects: the body darkens, smoke comes from the nose over half, a small readout "Damage 35 %", and **a little less power (up to 25 %) and top speed (up to 10 %)**; there is a hit sound (`SoundSystem.bump`, a burst of low noise, louder with the hit). **Switch off:** key **V** or `?damage=0`, kept on the device; the creative mode will switch it off by itself. The damage of other cars is not sent (their look is the same for everybody), only yours is shown.
- **The server's checks hold:** `tools/make-collision-poses.mjs` crashes cars with the game's own physics and contact code into the planter, the outer wall, a pole, head on and side on, at 15 poses a second; `CollisionRelayTests` replays all of them through the relay with the car park's walls and obstacles: **every pose is accepted, nobody is kicked** (largest turn between two poses 0.43 rad against the relay's 1.29, largest move 1.5 m). No contact gives a car a jump of more than 40 m/s (a safety stop); the fastest car hitting a car at rest gives it about 24 m/s, which the relay believes for the slowest car (tested). The hit flag in the pose still ends a drift chain and a clean sheet.
- **Not done / not checked:** nobody drove it with a real second player (contact and rubber-banding under delay are only reasoned and tested in pure functions); no contact between a car and the practice bot; damage is not part of the drift score; the numbers (restitution, friction, damage per m/s) are first guesses that need driving; the relay does not yet know about contact at all (it only checks that poses stay plausible), so someone can still drive through another car with a modified page, as before.

## Tandem (step 4, built 2026-11-26)

- **The judgement** (`game/sim/tandem.js`, C# port `src/Drift/Race/Tandem.cs`, shared vectors `tests/vectors/tandem.json` written by `tools/make-tandem-vectors.mjs`): two cars are in tandem while both drift the same way (slip over 15 degrees at over 8 m/s), at most 10 m apart, travelling within 0.45 rad of each other with slip angles within 0.4 rad; the quality q (0..1) is higher the closer (full at 4 m) and the more alike; the one ahead along the travel direction **leads**, the other **chases**, under 1 m along it is **side by side**. A chain of tandem seconds gives the **bonus multiplier** `1 + 0.5 q + 0.05 per second (to +0.5)`, at most x2, and bonus points `0.35 * speed * degrees^0.8 * (multiplier - 1)` per second (at most 1500), a break under 1 s is bridged, a chain ends banked.
- **On the server** (`TandemTracker`, hooked on the lobby relay's accepted poses): each car is judged against the nearest partner on the latest poses (nothing older than half a second); the car is told on a change, at most four times a second: `{t:"tandem", on, mult, role, q, with}` and `{t:"tandem.bank", points}`; the best banked chain of a signed-in person goes to the boards, **kind 3** (migration 012, `tandem` in `/api/times`); guests are judged and told but write nothing.
- **Tandem competition** (`TandemRun`, logic and board only, no page and no lobby messages yet): two rounds, A leads then B (3 s countdown, 4 s swap), the pair's score is the sum of what both earned: the lead its own slide rate, the chase the same rate times the quality while it is behind (a "chase" that is in front earns nothing); the pair's score goes to both signed-in drivers' boards, **kind 4** (migration 013).
- **Practice bot** (`game/sim/practice.js`): a bot that drives the orbit drift circle (found by trying: a steady circle at about 10 m/s on the car park without touching a wall) and the local judgement with the same functions; `?bot=1` or the key **B**; `?bot=near` starts it beside you; nothing is sent or kept. It knows only a circle: **an ideal line with angles per map comes with the maps of the open world.**
- **HUD:** a small chip "Tandem x1.4 - Lead" (`game/view/tandemhud.js`, texts German/English) and "Tandem bonus +N" when a chain is banked; the new HUD of the look session replaces the look, the messages stay.
- Tests: `TandemTests` (vectors, either side), `TandemTrackerTests` (lobby, told, banked, guest writes nothing, far and different ways earn nothing), `TandemRunTests`, in `game/sim/sim.test.mjs` the vectors, properties and the practice bot. The 64-driver timing test is loose now (p95 under 25 ms; it failed once at 10 ms while other test classes ran).
- **Not done / not checked:** the competition has no invitation, no messages and no page; roles in free driving are told but the client only shows the chip; the thresholds (10 m, 0.45 rad, 0.4 rad, the multipliers) are first guesses that need real two-player runs; the bot is a circle, not a line; the chip was not looked at in a real two-player session.

## Touge, slice 4 (zone scoring)
- `game/sim/zones.js` (pure, tests): clipping points (`clips` in the map file, here one at the inner edge of each of the eight hairpins, drawn as a white post with a pink glowing head) with a 9 m zone each. A drift through a zone pays when the car leaves it: value = (km/h / 10) x (degrees / 10) x 50 x closeness to the point; a clean clip (within 1.5 m) x1.5; each point pays once per run (R re-arms them). The bonus is added to the *pending* points of the drift chain (so the chain multiplies it and a hit or spin loses it); with no chain running it is lost. HUD: a "CLIP +N" or "Zone +N" toast.
- Client only: the server does not know the bonus (boards are paused), so a score with zone points is not comparable with the server's drift points. The line bot (a crude driver, 30 to 40 degrees, not on the line) earns about 170 to 300 per zone, a clean clip at 70 km/h and 35 degrees about 1800.
