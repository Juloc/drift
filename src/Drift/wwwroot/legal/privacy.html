What the game stores (for the owner's text) / Was das Spiel speichert

  • Your name and a password hash (no password, no e-mail address, no real name is asked).
  • Your garage (car, colour, nickname, tuning), your best times and your play statistics.
  • For sign-up limits: a keyed hash of your network address for 7 days; no raw address is kept. This is a pseudonym, not an anonymisation: whoever holds the server's database and its key could find the address again by trying all addresses, so treat it as personal data.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. No tracking, no advertising, no third parties.
  • You can delete your account and download your data in the account page.
